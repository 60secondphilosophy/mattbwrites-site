## Selected Approach: Approach 1 — Vintage Literary Warmth

This approach most faithfully replicates the original site's aesthetic: warm cream backgrounds, heavy condensed display type for the hero, alternating cream/dark sections, textured backgrounds, and prominent book cover imagery. The golden accent color on dark backgrounds matches the original's "WHAT READERS ARE SAYING" section perfectly.
